What one check looks like

Here is a real checkpoint from the standard: lap length in a first-floor column.

  1. 1

    The rule

    Where two bars overlap, the overlap must be at least the lap length on your structural drawing, usually 40 to 50 times the bar diameter (IS 456, Cl. 26.2.5). Here the drawing asks for 50 ×, so a 16 mm bar needs 800 mm.

  2. 2

    The value comes from your drawing

    Before the visit we load your column schedule, so the engineer’s tablet already shows 800 mm.

    Value loaded before the visit. The engineer measures; the app applies the rule.

  3. 3

    The engineer measures

    On site the lap measures 720 mm. The engineer types the reading and takes a photo with the tape in frame.

    QS-04-04 · Lap length
    Column C3 · First floorMain bar lap, from your column schedule

  4. 4

    The app decides

    720 is less than 800, so the check fails and the pour has to wait. The engineer can’t override it.

    Everyone knows, and it gets fixed

    The owner and contractor get the photo, the reason and the fix on WhatsApp. After re-lapping, we re-inspect free before the pour.

Questions

What happens if a check fails?+

You get the photo, the reason and the fix on WhatsApp. Once fixed, we come back and re-check it free.

When do I get the report?+

Within 24 hours of every visit. Every issue is graded minor, major or critical, with a corrective action list that names the person responsible for each fix.

Who are the engineers?+

Civil engineers with [qualification] and at least [X] years of site experience.

Do I need drawings?+

Yes. Checks such as bar size, lap length and cover are measured against your structural drawings. We load them before the first visit.

Which cities do you work in?+

Chennai and Bangalore.

Can the engineer pass something that fails?+

No. The engineer records the reading and the app applies the rule.
