Questions

What is the difference between Base, Premium and Signature?+

Base is drawn by an associate architect with 3–5 years' experience, with 2 revision rounds. Premium (architect with 8–12 years) adds a layout made for your specific site, detailed sections, the outside (elevation) design, one 3D view, approval drawings, 4 revision rounds and one site coordination visit. Signature is led by a principal architect with 15+ years, named on the drawings, and adds several design options, joinery and finish details, three 3D views, unlimited revisions until sign-off, and three site visits including a briefing for your contractor.

How is the price worked out?+

Homes up to 2,000 sq ft (ground + 1 floor) have a flat fee: ₹18,000 Base, ₹30,000 Premium, ₹45,000 Signature. Larger buildings are priced per sq ft of built-up area. Base: ₹9 for ground + 1 or 2 floors, ₹10 for ground + 3, ₹12 for ground + 4 or 5. Premium: ₹15, ₹16, ₹18. Signature: ₹22, ₹24, ₹26. Commercial buildings cost 20% more.

Can I add an outside (elevation) design or 3D views?+

Yes. Elevation design costs ₹10 per sq ft of facade area (width × height of the front), or ₹12,000 flat for ground + 1 or 2 floors. 3D views cost ₹5,000 for a day view, ₹9,000 for a night or aerial view, or ₹12,000 for a pack of three. Premium includes the elevation and one 3D view; Signature includes the elevation and three.

Can you also do the structural and MEP design?+

Yes. Most clients take all three so the drawings match each other.
