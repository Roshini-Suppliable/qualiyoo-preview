Purchasing

Buying steel, cement, tiles and fittings takes time, and it is easy to overpay or receive less than you ordered. We turn your material list into purchase orders, compare supplier quotes, schedule deliveries to match your construction stages and check every delivery against the order. There is no fee for the service.

No fees
  • Material list turned into orders
  • Supplier quotes compared
  • Free delivery scheduling by stage
  • Every delivery checked against the order

Why it matters

  1. 1Prices vary a lotThe same steel or cement can cost very different amounts from different suppliers.
  2. 2Short deliveries are commonWithout checks on delivery, you can pay for more than you receive.
  3. 3Materials dumped too early get wastedDeliveries timed to each stage mean materials arrive when they are needed.

Is this for you?

  • You are buying materials and want better prices
  • You are a contractor or builder without a purchase team
  • You want to be sure you receive what you paid for
