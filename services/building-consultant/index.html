Questions

How much does it cost?+

From ₹5,000 per consultation, which is a 30-minute call. Detailed field research is optional, from ₹10,000; request a quote, as it depends on the plot or building.

What is detailed field research?+

An optional site visit: an expert goes to your plot or building and researches it in detail, so the advice is based on what is actually there. It starts from ₹10,000; ask us for a quote when you get started.

Do I need plans or drawings first?+

No. The consultation is for before you start, so bring whatever you have, even if it is only an idea.
