Privacy

How we handle your information.

[ Draft — to be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before launch ]

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you provide: your name, contact details, project information and any documents you choose to upload. We use them only to respond to your enquiry and, if you engage us, to deliver our services.

What we collect

Enquiry details you enter in our forms; documents you upload; and analytics about how our website is used (pages viewed, buttons clicked and forms sent), including how you found us. We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel for this, which set cookies in your browser.

Who we share it with

With the qualified professional assigned to your project, and with service providers who help us operate (for example, email and WhatsApp message delivery, and the analytics tools above). We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to hello@qualiyoo.com.
