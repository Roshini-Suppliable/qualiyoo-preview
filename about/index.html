About Qualiyoo

Simplify your construction process. Know your building is built right.

Why we exist

Most buildings are built without anyone independent checking the work. Owners can't judge it themselves, and small firms can't afford every specialist. Qualiyoo brings those specialists together and shows you clearly what is being done.

One team, every specialist.

Structural engineeringFoundations, columns, beams and slabs.
MEP engineeringElectrical, plumbing, drainage and air-conditioning.
ArchitecturePlans, design and whether the site follows them.
Estimating & purchasingReal costs, the right materials, checked deliveries.

How we work

1Proof, not promisesEvery finding comes with photos, its location on the drawing and how serious it is.
2Follow it throughEvery issue goes on a corrective action list with the person responsible named, and we come back, free, to verify every fix.
3Plain languageEvery report is written so that someone who has never built before can understand it.
