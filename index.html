Questions

Where do you work?+

Chennai and Bangalore.

How is pricing decided?+

Each service shows a starting price. Design fees depend on the built-up area and number of floors. Site inspections are priced per visit, by building size. Purchasing has no fee. We confirm a firm quote before any work begins.

Is there a discount for taking several services?+

Yes. Book our services together as a bundle and get 15% off the total.

What size of building do you work on?+

Homes and commercial buildings up to ground + 5 floors and under 10,000 sq ft.

Do I need to pay to enquire?+

No. Enquiries are free and there is no commitment.

Do you replace my architect or contractor?+

No. We work alongside them, or fill the gaps they don't cover.

Who does the work?+

Qualified engineers, architects and purchase specialists. Where a drawing needs an official signature, it is signed by a properly licensed professional.
